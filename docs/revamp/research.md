# Research and live news

The deterministic screener ranks tradable companies by available financial factors. A rank only chooses what to research; it cannot authorize an order. Scheduled deep research prioritizes held names, then owner-focus names, then the highest-ranked remaining securities. Company enrichment is optional and uses up to 10 uncached requests for company snapshots per pass, prioritizing held and focus names; missing metrics are not invented. A material, timestamped news item may prompt a fresh held review or a new thesis for a focus or universe name.

## Owner focus list

The versioned `stock_agent/owner_focus.json` starts as `[]`. Add an entry such as `{"symbol":"NVDA","note":"Monitor earnings revisions","added":"2026-11-23","expires":"2026-12-15"}` inside the array. Use ISO dates and adjust them for your review period. Symbols on the active list are polled for news and included in the next scheduled research pass; they do not bypass quote freshness, broker checks, risk, or conviction requirements. The change belongs in version control and takes effect once the file is updated on the running instance.

## Decision and spend controls

- **Equity entries**: News-triggered buys and adds require effective conviction 8 or higher and raw conviction 7 or higher, plus the usual data and risk gates. An add also needs a fresh thesis strengthened relative to the last filled thesis. A bearish view can only go to defined-risk options checks, not to an equity short.
- **Model-driven exits**: A blind second review uses a separate provider when available, otherwise a different model on the same provider, with a degraded flag. A confirmation already in flight may finish after the close; if approved, it is queued for the next regular open plus five minutes. A live-news proposal still waiting to start its blind review expires at the close. A cap of four model-driven exits per regular market session is independent of protective stop processing.
- **Weekly spending**: The default budget is $15 per New York week, with 20% held back for held-position reviews and exit confirmations. Requests reserve a conservative configured estimate before they are sent. Unknown usage retains the reservation. A story seen on multiple feeds gets one completed first review; observed temporary failures can retry with backoff, up to three attempts. If the first review succeeds, a blind confirmation retry uses the original evidence rather than rerunning the first review. Completed proposals are recoverable after a restart without another model call.
- **Market posture**: The research posture switches to cautious when fresh SPY and QQQ returns are down at least 1% and 1.4% for the session, respectively. A posture switch can send an alert and raise the conviction floor, not start a trading job. Missing benchmarks do not trigger a switch.
- **Owner notes**: Provider sharing is off by default. If enabled, only explicitly approved, unexpired files with the prescribed front matter in `data/research_notes` are included as bounded, unverified context. They are not evidence, and the note directory should never include private account data or credentials.

## First-session entry audit

The research pipeline persists a fresh scheduled set only after coverage completes without required-input failures and includes at least one unheld, tradable name with healthy quote and news inputs. A healthy all-HOLD pass can satisfy catch-up; a stale pre-open pass cannot, even if the model returned only HOLD. A separate regular-session work scope can try the same symbols. In-session ranking prefers candidates with a usable quote without excluding held names or owner focus from analysis. A dated, citable feed story can ground a thesis directly; optional web search provides evidence when needed but is not an additional gate when credible dated input is already present.

Before proposing an eligible entry during the session, research refreshes stale quote or news proofs as needed. It preserves the original analysis price for the broker's drift check and does not change the raw/effective conviction thresholds. Scheduled bearish theses at effective conviction 8 or greater enter the defined-risk options lane independently of live-news limits. The durable `research.entry_funnel` event records the target session, screened names, theses written, conviction passes and explicit rejection counts.

The default direct model IDs were verified on 2026-11-24: OpenAI [GPT-6 Luna](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna) for primary research and [GPT-5.6 Luna](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-luna) for a distinct same-provider confirmation; Anthropic [Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Haiku 5.5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview) for the analogous roles. The offline scenario runs real provider adapters against fakes, including a Perplexity 401, a blocked FMP profile, and a Finnhub fallback after Alpaca news returns 403. It does not prove that any live credential is authorized or that real market evidence warrants an entry.
