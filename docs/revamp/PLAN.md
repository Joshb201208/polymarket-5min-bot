# Revamp plan

## Goals

The revamped bot will actively trade stocks, defined-risk option structures, and live market-hours news reactions on the paper account so the owner can learn, react to market themes such as artificial intelligence and bear-market swings, and pursue real returns with sized, transparent risk. Every trade will obey strict mechanical guardrails: stocks are strictly long-only with sized overnight Reg T margin borrowing (up to 200% gross long exposure protected by mandatory maintenance-margin excess buffers and automatic de-risking before any broker margin call), broker-side stop orders are the single stop-loss owner, defined-risk options are funded without margin and exit before expiry day, and the local ledger reconciles continuously against the broker. Research and intraday news reactions will run cleanly on the subscriptions that work today, trigger immediate catch-up research on first start whenever fresh theses are absent so valid buys can happen from day one, incorporate an owner focus list, and halt new entries loudly whenever required market data, news, or AI services are unhealthy. Daily and weekly Discord briefings will report honest broker-verified returns both before and after margin interest alongside unlevered QQQ and SPY benchmarks and average leverage, full option profit and loss, an explicit entry funnel explaining every buy and skip, active entry locks, and the live status of every data source. Done means the first-run account clean-up finishes automatically, every offline and continuous-integration check passes, and the bot completes live paper trading cycles with zero silent failures, zero unintended short stock positions, zero broker margin calls or forced liquidations, and books that match the broker to the penny.

## Principles for ordering

1. **Build offline test doubles and continuous-integration guardrails first** so every subsequent trading, accounting, options, and deployment change is proven offline without touching live broker or paid data services.
2. **Stop information leaks and deploy hazards early** by scrubbing credentials from files and logs, locking paper versus live broker settings, and gating the 10-minute server updater before new trading logic ships.
3. **Make the broker book and local ledger trustworthy before changing strategy** so reconciliation, fill polling, single-owner broker stops, and first-run account normalisation eliminate phantom profits and accidental short stock.
4. **Enforce risk, data-health, buying-power, and margin-buffer gates underneath active trading** so stocks, four defined-risk option strategies, live news reactions, and the owner focus list can trade actively from day one within clear, tested loss boundaries.
5. **Ensure zero manual server intervention at cut-over** by keeping code defaults operational with only the environment variables already present on the server today and keeping each item small enough to review and roll back cleanly.
