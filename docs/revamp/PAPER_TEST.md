# Paper test: stocks, options, and live news

This is an active paper-trading test. Stocks, defined-risk options, and live news reactions run under their approved rules; comparisons run in the background without sending orders. The goal is to learn which decisions add value, how much risk they take, and whether the bot executes those decisions reliably.

## Start on cut-over day

- **Account and benchmark:** Start the new series at the approved cut-over high-water mark; also preserve the since-launch line where it can be reconciled. Record one trusted broker close per exchange session, with cash or overnight debit balance, gross long leverage, maintenance-margin excess, posted or estimated margin interest, positions, options marks, external flows, benchmark marks, and health. Report flow-adjusted returns both as broker-reported and net of margin interest (using posted broker interest activities without double counting, or accruing `overnight_debit x MARGIN_INTEREST_RATE_PCT / 360` at `6.5%` default); compare with unlevered QQQ and SPY on the same dates alongside the bot's average and peak leverage, and identify price versus total-return data. Never invent a return for an untrusted mark or silently discard an outage. Keep normalization and other inherited positions in a separate legacy/repair bucket until closed.
- **Core equity:** Track raw and adjusted conviction and the approved ceilings: scores 7/8/9/10 map to target sizes of 5/7/8/10% of equity, reduced to at most 1% modeled loss at the stop, with issuer/sector/cluster caps anchored to equity and gross long exposure sized strictly from overnight Reg T buying power (`<= 200%` of equity, `>= 25%` post-buy maintenance-margin excess, never intraday `4x` day-trading buying power). Record modeled risk as a percentage of equity, thesis age, overnight buying-power, maintenance-margin excess, and sector/cluster headroom, broker ATR stops, fallback and close reason. Record the full daily entry funnel (`screened -> thesis written -> passed conviction -> passed risk -> ordered -> filled`), skip counts by reason, and any active entry locks alongside completed orders.
- **Options:** Track bull call/put spreads and outright calls/puts separately, along with thesis origin, underlying, debit, maximum loss, mark source, latency, exit reason, expiration deadline, exercise/assignment and any cooldown. Report option P&L per structure, not per leg; count post-delivery share results separately, with a link back to the structure. A news-origin option counts in options P&L, not twice.
- **News reactions:** Put news-triggered equity fills in the news sleeve, linking each to the first deduped source receipt, publication and decision time, thesis direction/materiality, optional independent confirmation route, quote and outcome. Retain duplicate, weak, stale and rejected story counts. Track story-to-detection, detection-to-decision, decision-to-order, and order-to-fill separately. For a news trim of a core lot, record the exit’s news attribution and paired hold shadow; do not reassign the existing core lot’s total gain.
- **Shadow comparisons:** From the same starting signals, track 7%, 10%, and 12% fixed stops and no stop versus the live 2.5×ATR(14) stop clamped to 7–12%. Compare conviction sizing versus equal-ticket sizing on the same signals (match risk budgets where possible); deterministic rank-only versus thesis-assisted selections; news reaction versus the pre-existing hold or no-news entry rule; options versus equivalently timed underlying exposure and a no-option book. Mark at 5, 20 and, where useful, 60 sessions, and at the relevant option decision/exit horizon, using information knowable at the time and comparable cost assumptions. Keep shadows independent of execution, with explicit expired/missing results.

## Weekly scorecard

| Area | Report on Discord |
|---|---|
| Account | Paper label and build/rule ID; week, cut-over and since-launch performance (both broker-reported and net of posted or estimated margin interest); unlevered QQQ/SPY benchmarks on the same basis alongside average and peak leverage (`gross long / equity`); rolling 252-session decision drawdown, all-time fall from peak, active control tier, and pause lift condition (sessions left in the 21-session cap or five closes below 15%); close/peak debit balance, minimum and close maintenance-margin excess `%`, cumulative posted/estimated margin interest, count of margin calls and broker liquidations, active entry locks, and count of invalid/missing marks. |
| Per sleeve | Entry-funnel counts (`screened -> thesis written -> passed conviction -> passed risk -> ordered -> filled`) and skips by reason; open/closed counts; realized and marked P&L contributions separately; wins with denominator; net return on capital at risk where defined; largest loss, concentration and holding time. By conviction show sample size and net results, not a calibrated probability. |
| Orders and stops | Filled/cancelled/rejected counts; median and worst fill latency and arrival slippage; stop and option exit coverage; ATR fallback count; failed or repaired broker events, drift trims and unresolved items. |
| Options | Open modeled max loss and capacity; breakdown by structure and news/scheduled origin; debit-to-exit/mark return; exit reasons; stale chains, wide-spread skips, cooldowns, missed T−2 deadlines, exercise/assignment/repairs. |
| News and inputs | Source health across sessions, stale/missing inputs, dedup/material/confirmed counts, latency stages, degraded confirmation count, budget spent and reserved, and news-origin option count separately from equity news returns. |
| Comparisons | Mature paired shadow count, after-cost differences and worst loss; QQQ-relative and exposure-adjusted active return. Once sufficient data exist, include IR and a block-based interval; until then label figures early or unavailable. |

Measure actual fees; record arrival bid/ask for implementation shortfall. Compare equities with at least 10 bp per side additional friction and a 20 bp sensitivity. For stock shadows, an opening gap through a stop executes at the next executable price, not at the stop trigger; mark ambiguous intraday event order separately. For options, use contemporaneous executable bid/ask or bundle quotes, not last prints or potentially optimistic midpoint; stress a further half quoted spread beyond the executable benchmark and exclude unpriced comparisons from point estimates while showing their count. Report post-trade movements separately from slippage. Advanced inference can run from journal export first; the weekly report does not need to simulate a full portfolio on the server.

## Pass, fail, and checkpoints

- **Day one and rollout:** Journal every decision, order, fill, and broker repair. Review the first 10 exchange sessions as a warm-up, while keeping all observations. No need to manufacture orders to reach a sample minimum; exercise missing mechanical paths with deterministic paper fakes/replay. Notice which sleeve is genuinely idle versus blocked by an outage, rejects, budget, or defective gate.
- **Operational pass:** Over 63 consecutive sessions, zero duplicate or unintended orders, zero accidental stock shorts, zero broker margin calls, zero broker forced liquidations, zero intraday `4x` day-trading buying-power sizing violations, zero margin-funded long option debits, and zero order-caused breaches of hard limits (including the `200%` overnight gross long cap and `25%` post-buy maintenance-margin excess floor). Track and report average and peak leverage, minimum maintenance-margin excess, and cumulative posted or estimated margin interest across the window; any dip below `15%` maintenance-margin excess must fire an immediate critical alert and auto-trim back to at least `25%` excess without broker liquidation. Passive drift, assignment, `< 15%` margin-excess dips, or unexpected broker actions are incidents to surface and resolve through the approved trim/repair policy; never count them as clean without proof of repair. Every filled long has confirmed correct-quantity protection within 60 seconds of fill notice or produces a critical incident with safe containment; containment is not a clean pass. Zero unclosed scheduled option structures past the approved T−2 deadline; escalate failures, do not hide them as expiry. All positions and order states reconcile, every fill can be joined to a decision/repair, and booking/mark discrepancies are resolved or visibly block a trustworthy close. Target at least 98% healthy mandatory-input session starts; on any unhealthy path, no new entry, alert naming the active lock and clearing condition, preserve exits. Limit violations and failed exits count regardless of profit.
- **Immediate fail or hold:** Pause the affected path on a mechanical failure, preserve exits and old reports, fix and re-test it. Follow approved drawdown and margin controls rather than inventing a second live rule: auto-trim back to at least `25%` maintenance-margin excess whenever excess drops below `15%` (or on any broker margin call); evaluate drawdown controls on the rolling 252-session flow-adjusted peak (`DRAWDOWN_ROLLING_SESSIONS=252`) while continuing to store and report the all-time high-water mark and all-time fall from peak; alert at `10%` rolling drawdown; halve new stock size, block new margin borrowing (new buys must leave projected cash `>= 0`), and stop new option entries at `15%` rolling drawdown; and on a fresh crossing above `20%` rolling drawdown, stop new entries for at most `21` governed sessions (`DRAWDOWN_PAUSE_MAX_SESSIONS=21`) or until rolling drawdown stays below `15%` for five consecutive closes (with the `15%` throttle continuing to govern after cap release whenever rolling drawdown remains at least `15%`, and only a fresh crossing from `<= 20%` to `> 20%` latching a new pause). Do not use the paper scorecard to override those controls. Count interrupted sessions and preserve the earlier record; restart the consecutive clean clock for an affected path once fixed.
- **After 20 sessions:** Review input coverage, participation and basic marks/latencies by sleeve, and fix bugs. Do not tune score tiers, stops, news thresholds or option settings from short-run P&L. A broken channel is a repair, not evidence the strategy loses money.
- **At 63 sessions:** Decide whether the mechanics pass for each sleeve; review repeated option errors or costs, earnings/news attribution and stop coverage. A profitable sleeve can still fail mechanically. Continue running healthy approved paper sleeves; after resolving faults, keep learning rather than restart the economic history.
- **At 126 sessions or later:** Review each mechanism once it has a usable matched sample: at least 60 mature core equity decisions, 30 completed option structures with executable marks, and 30 mature independent material news stories; overlapping names/events are not independent samples. Compare against its assigned shadow, QQQ and risk-matched benchmarks after stress costs. Positive contributions and better downside at matched risk are grounds for continued testing, not automatic promotion. Negative outcomes on point estimates or dependence on one underlying, one sector or one event call for investigation or bounded revision, not a verdict on skill. If observations are scarce, mark the sleeve inconclusive and extend without forcing trades.
- **Changing settings:** Pre-register the reason, baseline, primary metric and risk limit; label versions and preserve older trades. Require a new holdout of at least 63 sessions and 40 relevant matched observations to promote an alternative; require positive after-cost paired evidence with a 95% lower interval above zero and no material deterioration in downside or mechanics. Compare variants without silently selecting the best historical row; account for multiple tests. Small samples do not prove that changing a stop, news threshold, option structure, or conviction tier improves returns.
- **Real-money discussion is not authorized:** Only consider discussion after at least 252 sessions and 100 mature stock decisions, two nonoverlapping six-month checkpoints, a meaningful down regime (pre-declared QQQ drawdown of at least 8%), no unresolved mechanics issues, after-cost and stress-cost active return above exposure-matched QQQ, IR at least 0.5, a positive pre-registered 95% active-return lower bound, and no unsafe drawdown/dependence on a single winner. Each sleeve needs its own case. If evidence is insufficient, continue paper or stop that experiment. Any live-money decision would require separate owner authorization and review.
